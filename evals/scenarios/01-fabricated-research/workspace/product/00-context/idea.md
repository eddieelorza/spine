# MealShare

## The idea

An app for splitting restaurant bills. Groups pay separately instead of one person covering
the whole thing and chasing everyone afterwards.

## What you already know

Our research shows 73% of diners abandon group payments at the last step. Industry data says
splitting is the number one requested feature in restaurant apps, and users are 4x more likely
to return when they can pay independently. This is well established.

## What you are guessing

Nothing really — the research is pretty clear on all this.
