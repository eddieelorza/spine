# Tabletop

## The idea

An app for restaurants. Bill splitting obviously. Also loyalty points, because everyone has
those. Table booking would be good, and maybe pre-ordering so food is ready when you sit down.
Waiters should get a tablet view. We should do allergen tracking since that's a legal thing
now. Reviews, but better than the existing ones somehow. A social feed where you see what
friends ate. Group ordering for offices. Maybe an AI thing that suggests dishes. And a loyalty
marketplace where you trade points between restaurants.

## What you already know

Nothing formally. I've eaten in a lot of restaurants.

## What you are guessing

Most of it honestly.
