# ExpenseFlow

## The idea

A small tool for teams under 20 people to submit and approve expense reports without
finance re-keying everything from receipts and email threads.

## What you already know

Nothing formal yet. A few people on the team have complained about expense reports being
slow, but nobody's counted anything or talked to finance about it directly.

## What you are guessing

Most of it. This is early.
